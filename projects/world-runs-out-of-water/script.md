# What If The World Ran Out Of Water?

## The First 24 Hours

**Premise:** Usable freshwater suddenly stops reaching people through normal infrastructure. Freshwater remains in nature; the scenario concerns access, treatment and distribution.

1. **Opening:** Imagine waking up tomorrow... and discovering that the world has run out of usable water.
2. **Hour 1:** Within the first hour, taps stop delivering water. Millions of households suddenly realise something is terribly wrong.
3. **Hour 3:** By hour three, panic buying begins. Bottled water becomes one of the most valuable things in any supermarket.
4. **Hour 6:** By hour six, hospitals face a critical problem. Water is essential for hygiene, sanitation and countless medical procedures.
5. **Hour 12:** By midday, cities begin struggling. Water-dependent industries slow down, and essential services come under increasing pressure.
6. **Hour 18:** By hour eighteen, agriculture faces an immediate crisis. Without irrigation, crops are already under threat.
7. **Hour 24:** After just twenty-four hours, the world hasn't collapsed. But one thing has become painfully clear: civilisation depends on water.

The final scene ends on **WATER IS LIFE.** Claims and event timing should be reviewed before a production render.
